These archive entry pages were checked; every linked season and judge mirror was not individually audited. Pick a set with an accessible statement and analysis before scheduling it.

Suggested virtual progression

  1. After P01–P13, use selected approachable NAQ tasks for a short practice. If you read the mappings below, do not later count this same set as an unseen benchmark.
  2. Do a complete unseen qualifier-style set. Match its actual duration and rules; preliminary rounds are not universally five hours or one-computer contests.
  3. After the regional core, use a full regional such as NWERC. Keep the scoreboard hidden during the first pass; reveal it for postmortem calibration.
  4. Alternate your local/Asia West style with other regions. This broadens exposure without pretending their qualification thresholds are interchangeable.
  5. Attempt championships while still studying C modules; upsolve reachable hard problems rather than waiting to learn every technique.
  6. At the finals stage, alternate full World Finals virtuals with specialist drills and easier mixed contests for execution speed.

For historical PDFs, input/output conventions and judge limits may differ from modern mirrors. In particular, NEERC 2010 uses file names in its original packet. Follow the judge version you actually submit to. A statement link is not a promise of an active online judge.

NAQ 2022: five selections

Source: original statement packet. These classifications are deductions from the statements, not a reviewed official editorial.

Problem Preparation Why it belongs
C — Class Field Trip P03–P04 Merge already sorted letter strings; concatenation plus sorting also fits the small bounds
D — Ghost Leg P02 Apply adjacent swaps in the supplied order and report the resulting permutation
E — MazeMan P13 Grid reachability and component counting; preserve spaces and the special entrance rules
K — Smallest Calculated Value P01, P09 Enumerate operator pairs, evaluate left-to-right, and allow division only when exact
M — Toll Roads R08, C03 Bottleneck connectivity queries; DSU/parallel search or a reconstruction structure can also track component size at the threshold; process equal tolls together

Topi paper: two clearly matched local selections

Source: GIKI-hosted statement packet. The following methods are deductions from the given requirements.

Problem Preparation Why it belongs
02 — Whirlpool Trap Designer P08, P14 Required energy increases with integer radius; search the first trapping radius and compare with integer arithmetic as the statement recommends
03 — Air Attack Optimization R11 The requested roads are exactly bridges; handle disconnected graphs and identify parent edges by edge ID

Do not classify problem 01 “FFT” as a Fourier-transform exercise from its title: its described operation uses a maximum, not ordinary sum-product convolution. The local packet contains advanced tasks too, so “preliminary” is not a safe difficulty guarantee. These notes deliberately avoid inventing an editorial for the rest of the set.

Europe Championship 2025: four selections

Source: official packet and judge analysis. Associations are analysis-backed.

Problem Preparation Intended training focus

F — Mascot Naming P03, P10, C12 Subsequence impossibility checks and a greedy construction avoiding the forbidden subsequence

Two specialist exercises with explicit provenance

  • NEERC 2010 J — Jungle Outpost: the original packet, page 12 asks for a most resilient headquarters location within a convex watchtower configuration. Use W03; the half-plane-intersection reference explicitly lists this task. The statement and topic association are verified; a full judge solution was not audited here.
  • IOI 2016 — Aliens: official statement, for W02 after C04. This is an IOI task, included as a specialist transfer exercise. Study the cardinality-constrained DP, penalty formulation, tie handling, and hull optimization. The USACO Guide's Lagrangian-relaxation module is a supplementary explanation; use the IOI statement as the source of constraints and required interface.
